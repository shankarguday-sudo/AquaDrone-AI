🧬 Microplastic Detection & Classification Laboratory

AquaDrone compares transmitted and scattered light from a water sample to flag unusual particles. The following classifier is a demonstration of shape-based screening, not a validated plastic-identification model.

🔬 Stage 1 — Optical measurement

Measure the 0° transmission signal and 45°/90° scattering signals. Compare them with a clean-water baseline.

📊 Stage 2 — Feature extraction

Calculate normalized transmission loss, scattering ratios, signal variation, and—if an image is available—particle shape features.

🧠 Stage 3 — Classification

Flag a possible particle and estimate its visual or optical class. Confirm plastic identity with laboratory analysis.

Particle categories the system may screen for

〰️ Fibers

Long, thin strands. Image analysis can estimate length-to-width ratio. Natural fibers may look similar.

▪️ Fragments

Irregular, broken pieces. Shape, edge and image features can support screening.

▱ Films

Thin, sheet-like pieces. Transparency and orientation can change their optical response.

☁️ Foam pieces

Porous or irregular lightweight pieces. Bubbles and organic material can cause similar optical signals.

⚪ Beads / pellets

Rounded particles. Shape is best assessed using an image or microscopy, not scattering intensity alone.

❔ Other / uncertain

Unusual particles, mixed samples, bubbles, sediment or signals outside the calibrated range. Send these for further analysis.

Important distinction: These are morphology categories, not polymer categories. The three-angle red-light system cannot independently prove that a particle is polyethylene (PE), polypropylene (PP), PET, PVC, polystyrene (PS), or another polymer.

🎈 Balloon-Based Depth Control

🟡 ↑
Surface / Ascend

Inflate the balloon to increase buoyancy and bring the drone upward.

🟡 ↕
Controlled Depth

Adjust inflation and deflation to approach a target depth.

🟡 ↓
Descend

Release air to reduce buoyancy, if the overall vehicle buoyancy design permits it.

Engineering caution: A balloon alone may not provide stable depth control. Validate ballast, reserve buoyancy, pressure rating, pump and valve behavior, emergency surfacing and fail-safe operation in a controlled tank before open-water testing.
